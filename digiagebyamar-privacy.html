Privacy Policy

App: DigiAgeByAmar Pin Scheduler
Last updated: October 25, 2026

What this app is

DigiAgeByAmar Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the DigiAgeByAmar shop. It publishes the shop's own product images as Pins to the owner's own Pinterest account, on a schedule. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. The app does not read other people's Pinterest accounts, boards or Pins, and it uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Pinterest access

The owner connects the app to their Pinterest account through Pinterest's official OAuth sign-in. The app requests only the permissions it needs: to read the owner's boards and account, and to create and read the owner's own Pins. The app never sees or stores a Pinterest password.

How tokens are stored

The access and refresh tokens Pinterest issues are stored in a private file on the owner's server that only the owner can read. They are used only to publish the owner's Pins and are never shared with anyone. The owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings under Security & logins / Apps.
